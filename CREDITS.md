# Credits

## Original code this mod ports
- **[pokeemerald-expansion](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion)**
  by the RHH (ROM Hacking Hideout) team and contributors: Follower Pokémon, Overworld
  Wild Encounters, follower messages and scripts, and the behavior presets.
  See their [CREDITS.md](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/blob/master/CREDITS.md) for the full contributor list.
- **[pokeemerald-rogue](https://github.com/Pokabbie/pokeemerald-rogue)** by Pokabbie:
  the encounter chaining option.
- **[pokeemerald](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald)** by pret: the decompilation both projects are built on.

## Graphics
- Overworld Pokémon sprites, emotes, balls, shadows and field effects come from  pokeemerald-expansion's `graphics/` folder. They are the work of the expansion's sprite artists; see the expansion's credits for names.

## Engine
- **[gen1recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp)** by bryanthaboi and contributors: the LÖVE2D engine this mod runs on.
